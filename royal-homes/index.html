Common questions

Do I need to own land already?

No. We work with owners who already have a lot and with buyers still evaluating properties. Site evaluation is part of our scope.

Can modular work for rental buildings?

Yes. The same factory process covers duplexes, fourplexes, purpose-built rentals and larger multi-unit housing, with every unit on one schedule.

How customizable are the plans?

Start from a Royal Homes plan and tailor layout, finishes and fixtures. Fully custom drawings are also possible.

Where do you build?

Across Northern Ontario, from our offices in Sudbury and Timmins. If you're not sure your site is in range, ask.

What are you looking to build?

Answer a few questions and we'll come back with the right next step for your project — not a generic brochure.
